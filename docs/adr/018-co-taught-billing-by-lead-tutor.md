# ADR 018: Co-taught lessons are billed by the lead tutor; splits happen outside Penny

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-15

## Context

When several tutors teach one lesson, fee sharing depends on agreements Penny does not know about.

## Decision

Each payment request has one payee. For a co-taught lesson the lead tutor (whoever created it, unless the owner reassigns it) bills the whole fee and decides the split under the tutors' own agreement. Penny states this on the lesson and the payment request and does not track shares.

## Consequences

Billing stays simple and auditable. Penny cannot report per-tutor earnings for co-taught lessons.
