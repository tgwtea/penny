# Notice

A short inline message about the content it sits in. Flutter: `PennyNotice` with `tone` (`neutral`, `warning`, `danger`), a bold first sentence (`title`), an optional second sentence (`body`) and an optional action.

- Neutral (`surface-sunken`): information people need to act correctly, such as the co-taught billing note on a lesson and its payment request.
- Warning (`warning-subtle`): something to check that does not block, such as a clash while editing a lesson.
- Danger (`danger-subtle`): something failed. Say what and what to do; add a Retry action.
- The first sentence says the situation; the second says what it means or what to do. No apologies, no exclamation marks.
- Full tint with an icon. Never a coloured left border.
